# Radno vreme i izvoz

Existing hours and absence panels open a compact native dialog using the CRM design. The owner can export and enter time for any employee; the employee portal uses its employee token and server-enforced self access. No separate attendance page is added. Operational administrators can access only their own records through this endpoint. Clients and anonymous sessions cannot access it.

No migrations or backfills are required. Viewing/exporting is read-only. Saving writes only `attendance-v1:<core state ID>:<employee ID>` rows in the existing service-only state table; preview uses a separate namespace. Existing work logs, absence records, hour balances and authentication records are never written. Existing full-system backup captures these additional state rows through its existing stateRows manifest. Each employee document uses revision compare-and-swap; concurrent changes return 409. Each day retains all previous versions and actor/time metadata; no delete endpoint is provided.

Recorded start/end and breaks are local Europe/Vienna times, including next-day shifts. Net minutes use actual UTC elapsed time minus breaks. Ambiguous/nonexistent clock-change times are rejected; overlapping breaks, shifts spanning more than 24 hours and overlaps with neighboring days are rejected. The form asks for actual start/end times and all breaks. It does not create or modify activity minutes; there is no inferred historic schedule.

Hours exports distinguish gross presence, break minutes, net work, existing activity minutes excluding pause, and legacy activity pause minutes. Existing penalties and balance adjustments are not deducted. Missing records are explicitly labeled; absence days may have no time record. Legacy activity logs cannot prove start/end. Exports are filtered by selected employee and period (up to 367 days), limited to 1000 output rows; use smaller periods for larger teams.

Absence exports include original dates, statuses and working days intersecting the selected range. A separate vacation-balance sheet uses the CRM's existing entitlement semantics, initial usage and monthly overrides, as of min(range end, today). This is not a legal certification of historic balances or a new holiday entitlement policy. Underlying values are untouched. PDF embeds the existing Liberation Sans font; Excel is native OOXML with inline strings (formula-safe), numeric minute columns and frozen headers. No external export service receives employee data.

Tests: `node tests/attendance.cjs && node tests/attendance-api.cjs`; also registered in the normal fixture suite.

Inspection PDF: German, company and address, one employee per section, selected period, daily start/end, individual break intervals, net hours and totals. Incomplete legacy days are labeled, never reconstructed. Internal activities, notes, penalties and clients are excluded. Approved absences and existing leave balances have their own PDF; missing credited service and payroll information is explicitly identified. Excel retains the detailed internal reconciliation sheets.

Hours PDFs also show existing CRM activity durations per day and in total, excluding pause activities, with a separate source label. They are never added to actual attendance totals or presented as reconstructed clock times. An absent attendance total is shown as unrecorded rather than zero. Future days do not count as missing past records.

Fixed office schedules effective 2026-11-27: 30 hours = Monday–Friday 09:00–15:00 without a planned break; 38.5 hours = Monday–Thursday 09:00–17:30 and Friday 09:00–16:00, with a fixed 12:00–12:30 break. Current monthly contract history is read through the existing cost module. Positions containing Snimatelj (including mixed roles) and other contract hours retain manual times. No historical entries are backfilled. Prefilled times are proposals until the user confirms and saves the day. Actual extra minutes = max(0, actual net − scheduled net); they are a time comparison, not a payroll overtime classification, and never alter existing hour balances. More than six net hours with under 30 break minutes is saved truthfully with a warning. Break intervals are proposed as 12:00–12:30; the employee confirms actual use and can change the intervals before saving. A schedule snapshot is preserved with each confirmed record. PDF and Excel show additional minutes.

Simplified hours PDF: one working-hours column selects recorded attendance when present, otherwise existing activity minutes (marked *). Zusatzstunden show actual extras for recorded scheduled days and positive legacy activity-day surplus against the existing historical CRM target, excluding penalties, opening balances and manual adjustments. They do not reconstruct clock times or classify payroll overtime. Totals are displayed as the last table row and in the footer. Filename includes selected employee (ASCII sanitized), report type and range; team exports use svi-zaposleni.

Calculated clock-time proposals: for office employees with known monthly 30/38.5-hour terms and sufficient logged activity minutes, the report can display 09:00 and a calculated end including the planned break and extra work. Historical proposals use those known contract hours; filming roles, absences, non-working days and insufficient/missing activity are excluded. Actual clock-time fields remain empty until explicitly confirmed and saved. PDF marks calculated times †; Excel has separate calculated/source columns, and the UI labels them unconfirmed. Existing actual records always win.

Filming-position PDFs are a separate daily duration overview (Datum, Arbeitsstunden, GESAMT), without clock-time, break or additional-hours columns. Existing actual daily time is preferred; activity-only duration is marked *. This overview does not claim to certify missing actual clock times.


Final hour PDF format: office employees use Datum, Beginn, Ende, Arbeitsstunden, Zusatzstunden and GESAMT; filming roles use Datum, Arbeitsstunden and GESAMT. Durations are H:MM, never decimal hours (0:33 means 33 minutes). Dejan Klement is explicitly classified as office/scenario for attendance, without modifying his other CRM roles. Break records and calculations remain intact internally and in Excel, but there is no break column in the PDF. Calculated clock times remain labeled and existing actual times take priority.

Clean PDF presentation: no * or dagger cell markers and no explanation block below hour tables; GESAMT stays in the table. Calculated clock-time reports are identified in their existing title as Arbeitszeitübersicht (berechnet); activity-only reports use (CRM). Underlying source fields and confirmation state remain unchanged.
